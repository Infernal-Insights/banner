A GPU is granted once.

Turning GPU 0 on for Reef turns it off for Harbor. Cores can be split. A project that is pinned does not lend its devices out during someone else's rush.

A lull is not a failure.

When the queue is empty the container stays on the shelf and the work stops. If you opted in, those idle cores may run the project's steward. They may not hunt for a different banner.
