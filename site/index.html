Vote with your computer

Put your computer to work for something you believe in.

Banner is how people put computers behind a software project, a model, or a campaign. Finish a task and you have voted on what that project does next. There is no coin.

Choose a cause

A project says what it is trying to finish, and why your computer should spend time on it. You join that one. Nobody assigns you one.

02

Do a piece of the work

Your computer takes a task. A different computer does it again. It counts only when they agree.

03

That work is your vote

The more accepted work your computer has done, the more say you have in what the project does next.

Not a cryptocurrency

One computer, one vote. No coin attached.

Bitcoin began with a plain idea: people would vote with their CPUs. Banner is that idea aimed at a project, not at a currency. You are not mining. You are not buying in. You are contributing compute, and the record of that work is your vote.

What we left behind

A coin people trade.

A cryptocurrency pays you in something you can send, sell, and speculate on. The work exists to create the coin. Banner does not mint one, and your vote cannot be sent to anyone else.

What we kept

A record nobody owns.

Decentralization taught us that a project should not depend on one person, one company, or one website. Members keep their own copy. A second computer checks the work. If the founder disappears, the project is still there.

Why someone joins

The project has to be worth the computer.

People do not join because a token might go up. They join because the page tells them what will get finished, and they believe it. Your progress is finished work, not a promise.

Your machine

You decide what it gives.

Support more than one project. Set the cores for each, and turn GPUs on or off one at a time. When a project has nothing to do, your computer stops and goes back to you.

How the app works

If you have money to add

Money is optional. The vote is not for sale.

A project can accept ETH from a wallet such as MetaMask to pay for extra computers. That payment is not the vote. The vote only comes from work your own computer finished.
