Example project

North Branch water

A volunteer lab wants one year of river readings that someone else can reproduce. This page shows what a person reads before they decide to contribute their computer. It is an example, not a live project.

Why contribute your computer

A reading only counts if it can be checked.

The lab collects samples. Your computer runs one batch through the published method. A different computer runs the same batch. If they match, the batch is published, and your vote in the project goes up.

That vote is how the group decides the next sampling site, and whether the written instructions for the work should change. It cannot be bought, and it cannot be handed to someone else.

  • Publish a full year of checked readings.
  • Let the people who did the work choose the next site.
  • Keep every batch, and the method, public.
CPU is enough Quiet when the year is in

What your computer does

One batch, then it waits.

It does not run all night for its own sake. When the lab has no batches left, your cores are yours again. You can support a second project at the same time, with its own limit.

Joining

On a real project, Join adds it to the app on your computer. You then pick how many cores it may use, and whether it may use a GPU. This example has nowhere to send that work yet.
