Roadmap

The vote stays on the machine that worked.

MetaMask can sign a ballot and pay a bounty. It does not hold a bag of votes, because a vote you can transfer is a coin, and that is a different project.

The project does not live on the leader's computer or on this website. Every member keeps a copy of the charter and the log. New tasks follow the rules the members already voted, so a leader who is offline, jailed, or cut off from the internet is not a missing project. This site is a billboard. The copies are the system, and that part is not built yet.

Two keys

KeyLives inWhat it is for
Worker The desktop app, encrypted on that computer Signs the work. Sway accrues here, per banner. It does not transfer.
Personal MetaMask, or any Ethereum wallet Signs ballots and sends ETH into a project escrow. Weight is read from sway at the moment the ballot closes.

You link them once, each signing the same sentence. After that a ballot in the browser carries the fresh sway of the machines you linked. There is still nothing in the wallet to sell.

The project AI

Many machines in the project run one small model, the same weights, the same instructions, both pinned by a hash the members voted. This is copies, not one giant model sliced across houses. They draft in the lulls, when the real queue is empty, and only on devices the owner granted.

A draft counts if a quorum of those copies produce the same hash. It may slice the next piece of the stated goal into tasks, point at work that failed recompute, or draft a muster note. It may not accept a result, ban a worker, or post to a social network. The muster note stays a draft until a vote publishes it. The project page is how people are recruited. An empty queue is allowed to stay empty.
